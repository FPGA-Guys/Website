Unplanned downtime costs the world's largest manufacturers around $1.4 trillion every year. Only 14% of manufacturers have any prescriptive maintenance in place. The technology exists. It just isn't built for most of them.

MidMon is here to change that.

## Why we're building this

The enterprise predictive maintenance market is well served. Augury and Tractian have raised hundreds of millions to deploy managed-service platforms for Fortune 500 clients. Below that segment, there's a 30,000-plant gap in Canada and the US Northeast alone. Mid-market manufacturers with 50 to 500 critical assets who cannot afford a $1M deployment and don't have the staff to babysit it.

That gap isn't a niche. It's the majority of the market.

## What we believe

1. **The edge is the right place for inference.** Cloud round-trips add latency, cost, and a network dependency that doesn't survive a real factory floor.
2. **Self-installation is a feature, not a compromise.** If a maintenance technician can't bolt the sensor on in 15 minutes without calling us, we've failed.
3. **Pricing is a moat.** Below the threshold a plant manager can approve alone, the sales cycle collapses from twelve months to one. We priced MidMon there on purpose.

## What's next

Phase 1 runs through Q3 2026 and produces a working prototype sensor node validated on a real motor and pump. Phase 2 brings the first paid pilots with Quebec food and beverage manufacturers. Phase 3 is Series A.

More notes as we go.
